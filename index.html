About us

Small things, sorted

Little Labelled started with a crowded fridge door and a handful of vials that all looked the same. We designed our own name caps, pen bands and trays on our 3D printer, and now we make them for everyone who's ever squinted at a tiny label.

Everything is designed by us and printed to order in Queensland, in the names and colours you choose.

Choose your colours

Every item can be made in your choice of base colour and writing colour. Colour-code each name so you can tell them apart even without reading the label.

Made to fit

Tell us your vial size (2, 3, 5 or 10 ml) and your pen brand and model. If you're not sure, send us a photo next to a ruler and we'll work it out.

Questions

Good to know

Can't see your question? Email us and we'll get back to you.

Will the caps fit my vials?

Our caps are made for standard small glass vials. Tell us your vial size when you order, and if your vials are a different shape, send a photo with a ruler and we'll check before printing.

Which pens do the bands fit?

Our bands are sized for common reusable pens. Tell us the brand and model (or measure across the pen), and we'll make the band to suit.

How much is postage?

Postage depends on the size of your order. We'll tell you the exact cost with your proof, before you pay.

How long will my order take?

Everything is made to order. We'll give you a time frame with your proof, and let you know when your order has been posted.

Are the prices on the website final?

They're a guide. Some items start from the price shown, and your proof will show the final price. Prices are in Australian dollars. We're not registered for GST, so no GST is included.

What do you sell?

We make labels and organisers only. We don't sell, supply or give advice about anything that goes in your vials or pens.
